How the price is worked out

  1. Cost floor: your time (rate × hours) + a share of business expenses + job costs + unpaid sales time, plus contingency. Profit margin and payment fees are then taken as shares of the final price.
  2. Market check: the price is capped at the top of the competitor range you enter.
  3. Value check: your share of the value to the client sets the target inside that range: yearly value for projects, monthly value for retainers, value of the whole job for hourly and daily work.
  4. Result: the higher of the cost floor and the value target, so a quote never goes below cost.

Example ranges and costs are illustrations, not market data. Replace them with your own quotes. This is a planning tool, not financial, legal or tax advice.
